## A quieter capture interface

- Compact dark rectangular toolbar with no outline or shadow.
- Clear vector icons for pen, arrow, rectangle, numbered mark, Copy, and Cancel. Hover over a button to see its name and shortcut.
- Neutral tool selection, a blue Copy button, and shorter guidance keep the captured image in focus.
- Refined coral annotations with rounded pen and arrow caps, finer arrow and rectangle outlines, and compact numbered markers.

## Controls explained

The README now includes light and dark screenshots, a left-to-right toolbar guide, and mouse and keyboard controls written for everyday use. The portable ZIP includes the guide and its screenshots.

Existing shortcuts and gestures remain available: Ctrl+Alt+S to capture; 1–4 to choose a tool; double-click, Ctrl, Enter, or Copy to finish; Z/Y to undo/redo; and Escape to cancel. Copy the result, then paste it into your chat or document with Ctrl+V.

## Download and update

Windows 11 x64 portable build. Quit the older Myosotis from its notification-area menu, extract the ZIP, and run `Myosotis/myosotis.exe`. Otherwise, the older resident app will continue handling captures.

Scoop users: quit Myosotis, then run `scoop update myosotis`.

The executable is unsigned. macOS and Linux are not included. Screen-reader access to individual toolbar controls is not implemented; keyboard shortcuts remain available. Other DPI settings, mixed-DPI displays, HDR, and fullscreen toolbar contrast still need broader validation.
